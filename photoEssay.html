incantation one, a photo essay by brynn~

abstractions

this photo incantation interrogates oppression in the form of cis-hetero-patriarchal control expressed by the broligarchy in the ongoing sociopolitical nightmare colonizing Turtle Island and the bodies, minds, and spirits of the marginalized global majority, especially femme and queer folx of color. these visions offer medicinal missives for pleasure activism as an antidote against those colonizers especially who endeavor to keep us small by use of force, threats, violence, and politics to take our autonomy away from us. it is only those who are small themselves who need others to be disempowered. this is a loveletter to lavender menaces.

about this femme dark magic

these visions celebrate queer and femme algorithms for liberation. at its basest level, an algorithm is a recipe. some queer recipes involve cutting away from the system. some queer recipes require shiftiness that disallows others to pin us down. some queer recipes involve stitching ourselves back together like a community quilt or collage in ways those in charge will never be able to appreciate.

this is an ongoing project in early stages and is largely written in embodied energies as they engage in pleasure activism. activating pleasure is risky and flies in the face of the oppressors who want us down and out. they want our systems so submerged with trauma overload that we cannot possibly do anything other than to survive. the only way to fight back is through practicing the enactment of thriving. when we appreciate the things that make us queer and femme, the things that render us illegible to those who behave like our masters, we reinstate our own power.

this project is for the dykes, the queers, the femmes, the trans* and third gender folx, the neurospicy ones, anyone living beyond whiteness, and maybe also those allies who care to open themselves to the possibility that we have transcended presupposed coding and reality toward reworlding a way of life that is awake and alive to the embodied knowledge and intelligence they could not systematize out of us.

our aesthetics are poor copies of affirming everyday realities. we are distorted, disfigured, broken, assaulted, and raped. these are visual metaphors of self care and self love, pulling the pieces of ourselves back together, decolonizing, reclaiming, and rewilding our bodies and our voices. i speak with the many who don't or can't yet for whatever reason, but i know they are out there. i feel their blood boiling with mine and our many trancestors. we will be free.

    bio

    brynn~ is a queer worldmaker, eco-techno-feminist, and emergent strategist who centers care, unlearning, and transforming through play inside of abolitionist epistemologies. they design, facilitate, and improvise within queer playworlds, analog games, and interactive media art practices towards queer and femme communitas. this lowercase writing is a queer oral culture intervention into the many oppressions introduced by the medium of literacy.
